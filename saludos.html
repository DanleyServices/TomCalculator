Joke 01 // Attack in the Port
— Captain, we are under attack! — How many ships are there, cabin boy? — I see 10 frigates, 8 destroyers, and 4 battleships... — Oh no! Is it a fleet? — I wish, Captain! The bad thing is they all float and they are coming straight to ram us.
Joke 02 // The Blocked Port
Why can the ships of the artificial intelligence fleet never cast off? Because they always get frozen in port `localhost:8085` waiting for a response from the main server.
Joke 03 // Cloud Navigation
What does a systems programmer do when the fleet's ship starts to sink? They don't worry. They say it's a hardware issue and that they only work in the cloud.
Joke 04 // Digital Cargo
Why are modern navy captains obsessed with learning Docker? Because they were told that to manage the fleet of the future they needed to package everything in containers that float perfectly in production.
Joke 05 // The Fleet's Lighthouse
— Admiral's fleet here. Divert your course 15 degrees north immediately to avoid a catastrophic collision. — Lighthouse on solid ground here. Divert your course 15 degrees south instead. We don't float and we don't move!
