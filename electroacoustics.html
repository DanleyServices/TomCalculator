Equation 1: Speed of Sound
$$c = 331.3 \sqrt{1 + \frac{T}{273.15}} + 0.6 \left(\frac{H}{100}\right) 1.2$$
Result (c): 344.0 m/s
Equation 2: Wavelength & Period
$$\lambda = \frac{c}{f} \quad \Big| \quad T_{\text{period}} = \frac{1000}{f}\text{ ms}$$
Wavelength (λ): 0.34 m
Equation 3: Transit Time
$$t = \frac{d}{c} \cdot 1000$$

Equation 8: Vector Sum of 2 Signals

Summed Level: 0.0 dB
